## Full description
Bobo Land is a fun and safe games app for kids, with 39 games and no ads.

🏠 Bobo's House: collect the keys, hide in boxes and escape before sleepy Bobo tickles you! 6 levels, including a dark basement.

👤 12 games with Bobo: Memory, Letter Catch (ABC), Bobo Math, Colour Balloons, Bobo Jump, Bobo Runner, Block Tower, Fishing, Sliding Puzzle, Shapes, Fruit Catch and Where's Bobo?

🎉 Friends Party: 2–4 kids on one phone, each in a corner: Fastest Finger, Tap Race, Lava Floor and Tug of War.

⚔️ Two-Player Challenge: 22 one-vs-one games on the same phone: soccer, table tennis, pool, basketball, snake, car race, paint war, tic-tac-toe, rock paper scissors, bowling, archery, fruit slicing and more!

🪙 Collect coins and dress Bobo in hats, glasses and colours
🎁 A new gift every day
🏅 Sticker album
💾 Save code to move progress to a new phone
🌐 English, Arabic and Turkish (follows the phone's language)
✅ No ads, no purchases, works offline, collects no data
